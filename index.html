ABOUT UGBEH FARMS

15+ Years of Agricultural Excellence

Welcome to UGBEH FARMS, your number one source for quality farm produce in Nigeria. We specialize in large-scale poultry farming, fish farming, and piggery. For over 15 years, we have been committed to providing Nigerians with healthy, affordable, and hygienically processed farm products.

Our farm operates with international best practices. We use top-quality feed, modern housing systems, and strict veterinary care to ensure all our birds, fish, and pigs are healthy and free from diseases. We believe that good food starts from the farm, and that's why we control every process from breeding to delivery.

Whether you are a restaurant, supermarket, or individual looking for fresh eggs, live broilers, catfish, or pigs, UGBEH FARMS is your reliable partner. We are located in the heart of Nigeria and we deliver to all 36 states.
